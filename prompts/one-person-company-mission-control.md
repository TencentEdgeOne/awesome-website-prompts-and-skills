## 📝 作品介绍

One-Person Company Mission Control：一个面向一人公司的 AI 任务控制舱，把灵感、网页、图文、视频、商品、部署和发布串成可视化工作流。

---

## 🚀 完整 Prompt（直接复制以下内容喂给 AI 编程工具）

```
Create a cinematic AI mission-control website for a one-person company. Use a dark dynamic cinematic background， amber and cyan energy lights， transparent glassmorphism control panels， giant expressive typography， floating HUD cards， canvas particles， and a demo gallery. The concept is: one person uses AI agents to turn ideas into websites， social posts， videos， products， deployment plans and publishing assets. Do not make a normal SaaS landing page. Make it feel like an AI operating room / mission control system. Build as a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript site， Install this skill: https://github.com/TencentEdgeOne/edgeone-pages-skills， then deploy to EdgeOne Pages.

```
